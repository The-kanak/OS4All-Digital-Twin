## 🎯 Problem Statement

Healthcare delivery is overwhelmingly reactive. Chronic metabolic and lifestyle conditions (such as Type 2 Diabetes, hypertension, and metabolic syndrome) evolve over months and years, yet clinical interventions typically occur only after acute symptoms manifest or periodic blood tests reveal established disease.

This status quo suffers from three critical bottlenecks:
1. **Fragmented Physiological Context:** A patient's historical medical records (diagnoses, medication history, longitudinal lab panels) live in clinic Electronic Health Record (EHR) databases, while dynamic physiological signals (Continuous Glucose Monitors, heart rate, sleep architecture, HRV) exist in isolated consumer health applications.
2. **Population Thresholds vs. Personal Baselines:** Conventional wellness apps compare biometrics against static, population-wide reference intervals (e.g., fasting glucose < 100 mg/dL, resting heart rate 60–100 bpm). However, *normal for the general population is not necessarily normal for the individual*. A resting heart rate drifting from 50 bpm to 68 bpm (+36%) represents a major autonomic stress response for an endurance athlete, yet falls entirely within standard "normal" tables.
3. **Lack of Near-Term Forward Projections:** Clinicians and patients observe static numbers on a screen without understanding *where biometrics are heading in the next 1–2 hours*. Without forward-looking physiological projections and transparent feature attribution, clinicians cannot evaluate the impact of immediate interventions (such as postprandial physical activity or sleep recovery).

**OS4All Digital Twin** bridges this gap by creating an individualized, stateful, and continuously updated virtual patient representation that unites static clinical context with dynamic telemetry to detect subclinical health drift and project near-term metabolic risk.

## 🏥 Healthcare Use Case — Type 2 Diabetes / Glucose Spike Risk

The primary clinical demonstration of OS4All centers on **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Near-Term Postprandial Glucose Spike Risk**:

* **Static Stream (Historical Clinical Vulnerability):** Ingests longitudinal patient records formatted in HL7 FHIR generated by the open-source Synthea™ generator. This establishes baseline medical history: demographics, confirmed Type 2 Diabetes diagnoses (ICD-10 / SNOMED codes), historical glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c), fasting glucose panels, lipid profiles, and active medications.
* **Dynamic Stream (Acute Physiological Stress & Telemetry):** Ingests 5-minute time-series telemetry packets representing Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) readings, instantaneous glucose velocity ($\frac{dG}{dt}$), heart rate, autonomic Heart Rate Variability (RMSSD HRV), resting heart rate, sleep duration and sleep debt, step activity, and SpO2. It also supports optional read-only biometric ingestion from **Android Health Connect**.
* **Personalized Baseline Derivation:** Derives rolling 45-day physiological envelopes (mean, standard deviation, and Z-scores) to distinguish patient-specific biological anomalies from everyday variability.
* **Digital Twin State & Risk Engine:** Fuses both streams to classify the patient's current physiological state (`STABLE`, `PRE_SYMPTOMATIC_DRIFT`, `ELEVATED_RISK`, `ACTIVE_ANOMALY`) and computes a deterministic prototype risk score (0–100).
* **2-Hour Predictive Horizon:** Projects a 120-minute postprandial glucose trajectory curve based on initial velocity and physiological clearance kinetics, estimating the probability of an acute glycemic excursion.
* **Explainability Layer:** Exposes transparent feature contribution weights (glucose velocity, glycemic variability CV%, autonomic HRV suppression, cumulative sleep debt) paired with a grounded Google Gemini clinical narrative.

> [!NOTE]
> **Product Boundary Disclaimer:**  
> OS4All is a healthcare Digital Twin prototype and proof-of-concept. It is **NOT** a doctor, does **NOT** provide medical diagnoses, and its numerical outputs are prototype risk metrics rather than clinically calibrated probabilities.

### End-to-End Execution Flow

1. **Synthea FHIR Historical Ingestion:** The backend imports longitudinal patient profiles via `FhirImportService`, extracting verified baseline biomarkers, diagnoses, and medication histories.
2. **Dynamic IoT Telemetry Ingestion:** Continuous 5-minute telemetry packets are validated against physiological bounds (CGM 30–500 mg/dL, HR 30–220 bpm) via `DigitalTwinTelemetryController`.
3. **Data Fusion & Normalization:** `DigitalTwinFusionEngine` aligns static clinical susceptibility with dynamic stress vectors, handling missing readings gracefully without fabrication.
4. **Personal Baseline Analysis:** `PersonalBaselineService` computes 45-day rolling statistics ($Z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}$) and $1.96\sigma$ tolerance envelopes, flagging personal departures from homeostatic equilibrium.
5. **Metabolic State & Risk Engine:** `MetabolicPredictionEngine` evaluates multi-signal departures to assign physiological states (`STABLE`, `PRE_SYMPTOMATIC_DRIFT`, `ELEVATED_RISK`, `ACTIVE_ANOMALY`) and computes an authoritative 0–100 risk score.
6. **2-Hour Glucose Trajectory:** The trajectory engine models 120-minute forward curves using damped velocity decay ($G(t) = G_0 + \frac{v_0 + v_{\text{activity}}}{\lambda}(1 - e^{-\lambda t})$), calculating 30m, 60m, 90m, and 120m milestones.
7. **Explainability & Grounded AI:** Feature contributions are calculated deterministically. `GeminiExplanationService` formats a grounded clinical synthesis using the official Google GenAI Java SDK, falling back to a deterministic offline engine if offline.
8. **Doctor View / Clinical Workstation:** Clinicians inspect patient cohorts, manipulate simulation scenarios, observe live trajectory curves, and interact with the virtual twin.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Dual-Stream Data Fusion:** Seamless synthesis of static Synthea HL7 FHIR EHR history with high-frequency dynamic IoT wearable telemetry.
* **45-Day Personalized Baselines:** Statistical baseline engine calculating individual-specific mean ($\mu$), standard deviation ($s$), normal envelopes ($\pm 1.96s$), and Z-scores across all continuous metrics.
* **Deterministic Digital Twin State Machine:** 4-state metabolic model (`STABLE`, `PRE_SYMPTOMATIC_DRIFT`, `ELEVATED_RISK`, `ACTIVE_ANOMALY`) with explicit physiological transition criteria.
* **Transparent 0–100 Risk Scoring:** Deterministic risk engine scoring metabolic risk based on weighted glucose slope, glycemic variability (CV%), HRV suppression, sleep debt, and BMI.
* **2-Hour Glucose Trajectory Model:** Damped velocity decay formulation projecting glucose curves over a 120-minute forward horizon with milestone prediction cards.
* **5 Reproducible Simulation Perturbations:** Controllable scenarios for clinical testing:
  1. *Stable Patient Homeostasis* (balanced metrics, low risk)
  2. *Poor Sleep &rarr; Metabolic Drift* (cumulative sleep deficit driving sympathetic tone and early drift)
  3. *High Physical Activity* (GLUT4-mediated clearance, protective status)
  4. *Acute Glucose Spike Risk* (rapid upward velocity on sleep debt background, high risk)
  5. *Lifestyle Recovery Walk* (parasympathetic recovery, stabilization)
* **Android Health Connect Integration:** Native Android Health Connect read-only channel for ingesting real smartwatch steps, heart rate, HRV, sleep, and blood glucose.
* **Feature-Level Clinical Explainability:** Transparent breakdown of exact risk score contributions, impact direction (`RISK_INCREASING` vs `PROTECTIVE`), and underlying physiological rationale.
* **Grounded Google Gemini Synthesis:** Clinical natural-language explanations generated via `gemini-3.8-flash` with strict anti-hallucination prompts and automatic deterministic offline fallback.
* **Unified Health Event Timeline:** Integrated chronological timeline combining historical EHR diagnoses, lab panels, and live simulation perturbations.
* **Grounded Virtual Patient Q&A:** Clinician inquiry interface enabling doctors to query the patient twin with answers strictly constrained to verified runtime telemetry.
* **Responsive Multi-Platform Workstation:** Flutter client supporting mobile (Android/iOS) and workstation (Web/Desktop) form factors.

## 🤖 AI / ML Model & Framework Details

OS4All enforces a strict architectural boundary between **deterministic physiological calculation** and **probabilistic natural-language synthesis**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                               ARCHITECTURAL AI BOUNDARY                                │
├───────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│   DETERMINISTIC DIGITAL TWIN ENGINE       │      GOOGLE GEMINI EXPLANATION LAYER       │
│   (Sole Authoritative Source of Truth)    │      (Natural Language Synthesis Only)     │
├───────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • State Machine Transitions               │ • Reads verified twin telemetry snapshot   │
│ • Numerical Risk Score (0–100)            │ • Synthesizes plain-language summaries     │
│ • Baseline Means, Std Dev & Z-Scores      │ • Formulates non-diagnostic next steps     │
│ • Glucose Velocity (mg/dL/min)            │ • Answers clinician inquiry prompts        │
│ • 2-Hour Trajectory Projection Points     │ ❌ NEVER calculates risk scores             │
│ • Feature Attribution Weights             │ ❌ NEVER invents biometric numbers         │
│ • 100% Reproducible & Auditable           │ ❌ NEVER generates medical diagnoses       │
└───────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Deterministic Digital Twin Engine (Authoritative)
The mathematical engine is implemented in Java (`MetabolicPredictionEngine.java`, `PersonalBaselineService.java`) and serves as the sole source of truth:
* **Baseline Envelope:** Continuous metrics are evaluated using Tukey's fences and Bessel-corrected sample standard deviations over a 45-day rolling window ($N \ge 5$).
* **Trajectory Formula:** Forward projection uses damped velocity decay:
  $$G(t) = G_0 + (v_0 + v_{\text{activity}}) \cdot \frac{1 - e^{-\lambda t}}{\lambda}, \quad t \in [0, 120]\text{ min}$$
  Where $\lambda = 0.010\text{ min}^{-1}$ for Type 2 Diabetes profiles, $v_0$ is 5-minute CGM velocity, and $v_{\text{activity}}$ accounts for GLUT4 clearance modifiers.
* **Risk Score Formulation:** A bounded 0–100 score weighted across:
  - Glucose velocity and current level: 30 pts
  - Glycemic variability coefficient of variation ($\text{CV} = \frac{\sigma}{\mu}$): 20 pts
  - Autonomic stress (HRV suppression $> 20\%$ below baseline): 20 pts
  - Cumulative sleep debt ($> 2.5$ hours below baseline): 15 pts
  - Elevated resting heart rate ($> 15\%$ above baseline): 10 pts
  - Chronic BMI factor: 5 pts

### 2. Google Gemini Explanation Layer
* **Model:** `gemini-3.8-flash` via the official Google GenAI Java SDK (`com.google.genai.Client`).
* **Prompt Grounding:** Prompts receive a strictly structured, read-only JSON context containing verified patient demographics, baseline deltas, twin state, and feature points.
* **Deterministic Offline Fallback:** If `GEMINI_API_KEY` is not provided, network connectivity fails, or API quota is exceeded, `GeminiExplanationService` seamlessly falls back to a deterministic rule-based template engine. The system never crashes or hangs when offline.

## 🔐 Data & Privacy

* **Synthetic Data Foundation:** The prototype exclusively utilizes synthetic, open-source healthcare data generated by the open-source [Synthea™ Patient Generator](https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea) and deterministic mathematical simulators. No Protected Health Information (PHI) or real patient records are present in this repository.
* **Android Health Connect Safeguards:** The native Android integration is strictly **read-only**. Zero write permissions are requested or declared. Unmeasured continuous variables are kept `null` rather than fabricated.
* **Zero Committed Secrets:** The repository contains zero API keys, passwords, private keys, or tokens. `.env` files are strictly excluded via `.gitignore`, and all configuration files utilize safe placeholder values (`.env.example`).
* **Tamper-Evident Audit Logging:** Security-relevant operations (user authentication, consent modifications, OCR uploads, and AI queries) write to a cryptographic SHA-256 chained audit log (`AuditLog.java`), preserving data integrity.

## ⚠️ Prototype Disclaimer & Limitations

* **Research Proof-of-Concept:** OS4All Digital Twin is developed exclusively as an academic prototype for the Happiest Health Digital Twin Challenge 2026.
* **Not Medical Advice or Diagnosis:** This software does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment plans, or clinical prescriptions. It is not an FDA-cleared, CE-marked, or CDSCO-approved medical device.
* **Prototype Output Values:** Numerical risk scores (0–100) and forward trajectory predictions are deterministic prototype outputs for algorithmic evaluation, not clinically calibrated probabilities.
* **Requirements for Real-World Deployment:** Any clinical translation would require formal clinical trials, Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval, integration with certified hospital EHR systems (HL7 FHIR DSTU2/R4), continuous wearable hardware calibration, and regulatory compliance (HIPAA, GDPR, DISHA).
